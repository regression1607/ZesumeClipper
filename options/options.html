Zesume Clipper settings

Clip a job description from any job posting, then tailor one of your Zesume.ai resumes to it in one click. You stay signed in through your normal Zesume session — no API keys are stored.

Your Zesume backend — serves /api/* and holds your login session. Default: https://resume-backend-iota-ten.vercel.app.

Your Zesume web app — where tailored resumes open (/r/:id, /dashboard). Default: https://zesume-frontend.vercel.app. For local dev use http://localhost:5000 (API) and http://localhost:5173 (app).

Auto-apply on LinkedIn / Indeed fills application fields using Zesume's own AI through your signed-in account — no API key needed. It uses the details below to answer name/email/experience questions. Everything stays in this browser.

  1. Open a job posting (LinkedIn, Indeed, Greenhouse, Lever, or most career pages).
  2. Click the Zesume Clipper icon — it reads the job description.
  3. Pick a resume and hit Tailor. The tailored resume opens in Zesume.

Not signed in? The popup can still copy the JD and open Zesume so you can paste it in.
